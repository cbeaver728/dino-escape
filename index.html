Dino EscapeGet to the Base

Pitch dark. You have headlights and a compass, and that is it. Somewhere past the treeline is a fenced base with a helipad. Reach the pad and you live.

  • Six T-Rexes roam the map. They plod along until they hear your engine - the faster you drive, the further the sound carries.
  • Once one locks on it sprints at 1.3x your top speed. You cannot outrun it in a straight line. Use the trees: it turns wide.
  • It sprints in bursts, then has to catch its breath. That is your window.
  • Kill the engine and you go dark and silent - it can only hunt where it last heard you. Do it early, and in among the trees. Do it late, or out in the open, and it walks straight to you.
  • Water slows you - and slows them more.
  • New map every single game.
